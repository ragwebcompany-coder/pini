02 Areas of Expertise

Services I offer

A personalised approach without strict diets — we focus on what to add to your diet, not only on what to take away. Select a service to read more.

01

Personalised Nutritional Assessment

Medical history, body composition analysis, and a tailored dietary plan.
02

Clinical Nutrition

Specialised protocols for insulin resistance, diabetes mellitus, elevated lipids (cholesterol/triglycerides), gastrointestinal disorders (e.g. irritable bowel syndrome) and hormonal disorders (e.g. Hashimoto’s disease, polycystic ovary syndrome).
03

Nutritional Support for GLP-1 Therapies

A specially designed programme for individuals undergoing weight-loss treatment, aimed at maintaining muscle mass, ensuring adequate nutrient intake and managing side effects.
04

Nutrition during the Menopausal Transition – Menopause

A specialised nutritional programme to manage hormonal fluctuations and support weight management.
05

Sports Nutrition

Optimising athletic performance, planning pre- and post-workout meals, and hydration strategies.
06

Nutrition for Fertility, Pregnancy and Breastfeeding

Specialised support for fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding.
07

Nutrition for Irritable Bowel Syndrome

Targeted dietary interventions to manage IBS symptoms, such as abdominal pain, bloating, flatulence, diarrhoea or constipation.
08

Counselling for Emotional Eating

Training to break free from the mindset of restrictive dieting, manage emotional overeating and improve one’s relationship with one’s body.
09

Online Nutrition Coaching

Fully remote support via video call sessions for those with a busy schedule or living abroad.
10

Corporate Wellness

Wellness programmes for companies (interactive workshops, office nutrition talks, one-to-one sessions for employees).
